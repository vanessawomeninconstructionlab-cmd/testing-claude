# Weekly Painting Bid Scan — Zip 33332

Weekly reports of open government and private painting bid opportunities (NAICS 238320)
within reach of Southwest Ranches, FL 33332 and surrounding Broward County.

## How it works
- Every Monday, a scheduled Claude session researches current open solicitations across
  the portal watchlist below, then writes a dated report to this folder and pushes it to
  the branch `claude/weekly-painting-bids-scanner-nt0pvd`.
- Reports are named `YYYY-MM-DD-painting-bid-scan-33332.html`. The newest file is the
  current week's document.

## Portal watchlist (sources scanned)
Government: Town of Southwest Ranches (DemandStar), Broward County BPRO (Bonfire),
Broward County Public Schools (Public Purchase), Pembroke Pines (OpenGov), Miramar,
Cooper City, Davie, Weston (DemandStar), Fort Lauderdale (Public Purchase), Tamarac
(Bids & Tenders), Broward County Housing Authority (Vendor Registry), Broward Health,
Memorial Healthcare, Broward College, Miami-Dade County (INFORMS) + MDCPS,
State of Florida (MyFloridaMarketPlace), Federal (SAM.gov — NAICS 238320, FL).

Private: PlanHub, ConstructConnect, BuildingConnected, The Blue Book, DemandStar
metro browse, HOA/condo management firms (FirstService Residential, Castle Group,
KW Property Management), and South Broward GC prequalification lists.

## Note on verification
Most portals gate bid documents behind free vendor registration, so each report flags
leads that need a deadline confirmed on the portal before pursuing.
