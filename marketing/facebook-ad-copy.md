## 🎯 Suggested targeting (Ads Manager)

- **Goal / Objective:** Leads or Sales → optimize for *Landing page views* or *Leads*.
- **Location:** your service area — a radius (e.g. 15–25 miles) around your town/ZIP.
- **Age:** 30–65+ (homeowners).
- **Detailed targeting (optional):** Homeowners; interests like *Home improvement*,
  *This Old House*, *HVAC*, *Home ownership*. Keep it broad — Meta's algorithm
  finds buyers if your creative + offer are strong.
- **Placements:** Advantage+ (automatic) is fine to start; the images fit Feed,
  Reels, and Stories.
- **Budget:** start small — **$10–20/day** for 4–7 days, then scale the winner.

## 💡 Tips to get the most out of it

1. **Run all 3 primary-text versions** as separate ads in one ad set; kill the
   losers after ~50–100 clicks and put budget on the winner.
2. **Make sure the link goes straight to booking** so the ad → book → pay path
   is seamless (that's what your CoolAir page is built for).
3. **Refresh the offer seasonally:** swap "$89 A/C Tune-Up" for
   "$89 Furnace Tune-Up & Safety Check" in fall/winter (the app already has it).
4. **Add a "Book Now" button** on your Facebook Page too, pointing to the same link.
5. **Reply fast.** Speed-to-lead wins HVAC jobs — the online booking helps, but
   follow up on any questions quickly.
